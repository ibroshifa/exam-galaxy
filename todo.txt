Exam Galaxy — Ethiopian Entrance Exam & Study Prep App

Exam Galaxy is a purpose-built educational app designed to help Ethiopian students prepare effectively for national exams, especially the Ethiopian University Entrance Examination (EuEE) and related academic assessments. It offers a rich collection of past exam questions, detailed explanations, chapter-based practice, revision notes, and study tools — all in one easy-to-use mobile app.

At its core, Exam Galaxy provides comprehensive exam preparation resources that align with the Ethiopian curriculum. The app includes thousands of past questions from entrance exams (2000–2017 Ethiopian Calendar) and Grade 10 matric exams (2003–2008), each with correct answers and clear, detailed explanations to help students not just test themselves but understand why the answers are correct. 
Chrome Stats

One of the app’s key strengths is its organizational structure. Questions are categorized by subject, unit, and chapter, allowing learners to focus on specific topics or weaknesses systematically. This makes it ideal for students who want targeted practice, whether they’re revising individual subjects like Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, English, History, or preparing for full-length entrance examinations. 
Exam Galaxy

Exam Galaxy also includes short revision notes based on official Ethiopian Curriculum textbooks for grades 9 through 12. These concise summaries help students review key concepts quickly and efficiently, saving time and reinforcing understanding ahead of exams. 
Exam Galaxy

To support consistent study habits, the app features a built-in study timer and time tracker, letting students log their study sessions, manage their focus periods, and maintain a regular revision schedule. Its user-friendly interface is designed for smooth navigation, with all essential features easily accessible so that learners of all levels can use the app without confusion.

Whether you are a Grade 12 student preparing for university entrance, a lower-grade student building foundational knowledge, or a teacher looking for structured practice materials, Exam Galaxy helps you study smarter and with confidence. Download the app to access a vast library of practice questions, thorough explanations, topic-based practice, and quick revision notes — all tailored to help you succeed academically.
